How it works

  1. Gather your evidence. Have your grant number, award amount, beneficiary figures and deliverable evidence ready.
  2. Complete the form. Sign in to Google if you want to save progress and come back later.
  3. Upload files where asked. Supporting evidence is uploaded inside the form.
  4. Submit and get a copy. You'll get a confirmation email with your answers.
Data protection: Do not submit personal participant data or details of safeguarding cases. Report numbers and anonymised examples only.
